We make things people want to spend time in.

Maven Studio is an independent production house working where film and interactive worlds meet. We write, shoot, and edit cinematic video — and we design, build, and ship experiences on Roblox, from original games to wearable drops.

Different mediums, same standard: every project gets the same attention to craft, whether it ends up in a theater or on an avatar.

Three crafts, one studio.

Film Production

Short films, brand films, and commercial work — shot, directed, and edited in-house from first frame to final cut.

Roblox Games

Original multiplayer games built for Roblox, designed for replay value and built to hold up at scale.

Roblox Clothing

UGC apparel and accessories for Roblox avatars, designed in-house and sold through the Roblox marketplace.
